# Zaiblify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

Zaiblify is an offline text-formatting app by Zaib Labs.

## Data processing

- Text entered into Zaiblify is processed locally on the device.
- Zaiblify does not require an account.
- Zaiblify does not send formatted text to Zaib Labs servers.
- Clipboard access occurs only when the user taps Paste or Copy.

## Advertising

Advertisements are not enabled in the current release. If advertising is introduced in a future release, the app will provide an appropriate consent flow before personalized advertising and will update this policy.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Zaib Labs through the support channel listed on the official app listing.
